Mockups

Four rough takes on what could make Sphere Pool fun. Each is a 2-minute 4v4 match on a tall pitch. Play one match of each, then tell Claude: did you want a rematch, did you feel clever, did anything make you react out loud, and did it ever feel like waiting?

A · no menus

Slipstream

Play never stops. Hold your thumb down to slow time and pull back to aim a pass or shot through the gaps.

 B · Blitzball, done right

Lanes

A few stops, each a puzzle: every pass and shot shows how much survives the distance and the defenders in its way.

 C · play the moments

Showdowns

The match flows by itself; you call tactics and play the big moments, reading an opponent that learns your habits.

 D · tug of war

Tide

Quick Safe-or-Bold duels push the tide line toward their goal, and the heat builds with every clean exchange.
